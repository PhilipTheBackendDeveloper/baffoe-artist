Fine Art & Custom Commissions

Original bespoke paintings in acrylic, oil, and mixed media on premium Belgian linen and archival canvas. From intimate collector portraits to expansive cultural centerpieces, each commission is crafted with deep narrative, gold leaf accents, and layered textural depth.

Acrylic on Canvas Oil Painting Bespoke Portraits 24K Gold Leaf Archival Materials Private Collectors Museum Quality

Live Event & Gala Painting

Electrifying live art performances where Baffoe creates a complete, museum-worthy painting in real time before your guests. Ideal for luxury weddings, high-profile galas, corporate anniversaries, and cultural festivals in Accra and globally.

Weddings & Anniversaries Corporate Galas Real-Time Performance Guest Engagement Same-Day Presentation Charity Auctions VIP Entertainment

Murals & Architectural Installations

Transformative large-scale wall art for boutique hotels, upscale restaurants, corporate headquarters, and luxury private estates. Baffoe combines traditional Ghanaian motifs with contemporary architectural lines to create unforgettable spatial identity.

Interior Murals Exterior Architecture Spatial Identity Hospitality Spaces Corporate Art Weatherproof Pigments Site-Specific Scale

3D Modeling & Spatial Design (Rhino & Blender)

Leveraging advanced digital design tools (Rhinoceros & Blender) for 3D conceptual modeling, digital sculptures, architectural pre-visualizations, and innovative CGI creative directions rooted in fine art principles.

Rhinoceros 3D Blender Sculpting Architectural Pre-Viz Digital Artworks Spatial Form Studies CGI Asset Creation KNUST Foundation
