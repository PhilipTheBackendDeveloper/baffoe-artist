FAQ

Collector FAQ

How do I commission an original artwork from Baffoe?

Initiate an inquiry via WhatsApp or our contact form detailing your preferred medium, size, and concept. Baffoe will schedule a consultation, provide concept sketches and palette swatches, and begin execution once details are finalized.

How does live painting work at weddings, galas, and corporate events?

Baffoe arrives early to set up his easel, canvas, and studio materials. Throughout the event, guests witness the artwork being created in real time, capturing the celebration's energy. The finished artwork is presented to the hosts or auctioned for charity.

Can original paintings be crated and shipped internationally from Accra?

Yes. All international shipments are professionally packed in custom wooden crates, fully insured, and shipped via trusted art couriers (DHL/FedEx Art Services) with full tracking and customs clearance support.

How does Baffoe incorporate 3D modeling (Rhino & Blender)?

As a KNUST-trained designer, Baffoe uses Rhino and Blender for spatial pre-visualization, digital sculpting, and architectural mural mapping. This allows clients to preview exact proportions and lighting in their physical space before painting begins.
