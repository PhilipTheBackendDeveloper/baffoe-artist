Collector & Booking FAQ

Everything you need to know
about commissioning & booking

Comprehensive answers on private canvas commissions, live event painting performances, architectural murals, 3D spatial design, and international crating from Accra.

How do I commission an original artwork from Baffoe?

Initiate an inquiry via WhatsApp (+233 54 045 5082) or our contact form. Share your desired size, medium (acrylic, oil, mixed media, charcoal), and any thematic concepts. Baffoe will consult with you, provide concept sketches and palette swatches, and begin painting once the contract and deposit are confirmed.

How does booking Baffoe for live painting at weddings and galas work?

Baffoe arrives 1-2 hours prior to the event to assemble his easel, canvas, and palette. Over the course of 4 to 8 hours, guests enjoy the spectacle of seeing the canvas come alive with dynamic brushwork and vibrant energy. At the conclusion of the event, the artwork is presented to the newlyweds, hosts, or auctioneer.

Can Baffoe design and paint large commercial and residential murals?

Yes. Baffoe has executed transformative indoor and outdoor murals for corporate offices, restaurants, hotels, and luxury private residences across Ghana. Murals can incorporate custom branding or cultural motifs and are executed with weather-resistant, archival pigments.

How does Baffoe integrate 3D modeling (Rhino & Blender) into his work?

Having graduated from KNUST with formal training in fine art and spatial design, Baffoe uses Rhinoceros and Blender for 3D modeling, digital sculptural exploration, and virtual architectural mapping before laying physical paint on monumental surfaces.

What is the typical completion timeline and payment structure?

Private canvas commissions typically take 3 to 6 weeks depending on scale and drying times for oil/acrylic layers. Payment is structured in standard art milestones: 50% deposit upon concept agreement, and the remaining 50% upon final collector approval prior to crating/delivery.

Can artworks be crated and shipped internationally from Ghana?

Absolutely. Original canvases are professionally packed in museum-standard custom wooden crates with waterproof and impact-resistant cushioning. We ship via express international art couriers (DHL/FedEx Art Logistics) with full insurance, door-to-door delivery, and customs clearance support.

Do original artworks come with a Certificate of Authenticity?

Yes. Every original painting and sculpture includes an official Certificate of Authenticity hand-signed by Baffoe Kwabena, noting the title, medium, exact dimensions, completion date, and unique studio registry index number.
