About the Artist

From Canvas to 3D: Stories in Raw Emotion

Baffoe Kwabena is a multidisciplinary Ghanaian visual artist and 3D designer based in Accra. A graduate of Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST) with nearly a decade of dedicated studio practice, his work bridges traditional fine art — rich acrylics, textured oils, charcoal, and sculpture — with cutting-edge 3D modeling and visualization in Rhino and Blender. He accepts private commissions, creates monumental murals, and paints live at high-profile events and celebrations.

What art mediums and disciplines does Baffoe specialize in?

Baffoe Kwabena is a multidisciplinary artist working in acrylics, oils, pencil and charcoal, mixed media, sculpture, and large-scale murals, as well as digital 3D modeling and rendering in Rhino and Blender. His formal background from KNUST allows him to navigate seamlessly between classical fine art and futuristic digital design.

How do I commission a custom original painting or portrait?

You can begin by messaging Baffoe directly on WhatsApp at +233 54 045 5082 or submitting the booking form below. Share your concept, preferred dimensions, room aesthetic, and desired delivery date. Baffoe will consult on the vision, propose color palettes and sketches, and upon approval begin creation with regular progress updates.

Can I book Baffoe for live painting at my wedding or corporate gala?

Yes! Live event painting is one of Baffoe's premier specialties. He sets up an easel live at your venue in Accra or internationally, painting the scene, couple, or event atmosphere as it unfolds. It creates unforgettable entertainment for guests and leaves you with an original heirloom artwork completed on your special day.

How long does a commissioned artwork take to complete?

Most canvas paintings take between 2 to 4 weeks depending on size, technique, and medium (oil paintings require additional curing and varnishing time). Murals and live event bookings depend on project scope and event dates. Expedited timelines for special milestones can be accommodated upon request.

Do you ship original artworks internationally?

Yes, original artworks are shipped worldwide from Accra, Ghana. Each piece is packaged using museum-grade protective layering and shipped either stretched in custom wooden crates or rolled in heavy-duty tubes, fully insured with door-to-door tracking.

Does each artwork come with a Certificate of Authenticity?

Every original artwork and limited edition print is signed by Baffoe Kwabena and accompanied by an official Certificate of Authenticity specifying the title, year, medium, dimensions, and provenance.

Can Baffoe collaborate on 3D design and commercial mural projects?

Absolutely. Baffoe works closely with interior designers, architects, real estate developers, and brands to create site-specific murals and 3D Rhino/Blender concept assets that elevate commercial and residential spaces.

Sankofa Resonance — Original Artwork

Project Page

Private Gallery Collection

Sankofa Resonance represents an exploration of Akan cultural heritage through rich impasto textures, vivid contemporary acrylic tones, and delicate 24K gold leaf detailing. Created in Baffoe's Accra studio.

Challenge

The core creative challenge of Sankofa Resonance was harmonizing the delicate spiritual radiance of genuine 24K gold leaf with the tactile power of heavy oil impasto and raw Belgian linen. Balancing Akan ancestral cosmology with contemporary spatial minimalism required months of dedicated studio experimentation in Accra.

Equally demanding was translating computational 3D geometries developed in Rhino and Blender into physical painterly brushwork, ensuring that mathematical precision never superseded the vital, living human hand.

Studio Disciplines

Belgian Linen & Impasto

Heavy palette-knife relief work with custom organic earth pigments, cold-pressed linseed oils, and natural binders for rich sculptural presence.

24K Gold Leaf Guilding

Sacred Akan Adinkra motifs applied with historic hand-gilding techniques, creating divine luminosity under gallery and residential lighting.

Rhino & Blender 3D

Computational 3D spatial modeling used to pre-calculate dimensional relief, lighting angles, and architectural sightlines.

Solution

Baffoe Kwabena brings together a museum-caliber collection of contemporary African fine art, live event paintings, and monumental spatial murals. Rooted in Akan symbolism, rich Belgian linen, and raw emotional depth, each piece tells a living story.

Acquired by private collectors across Ghana, the UK, and the United States, the series stands as a landmark testament to the dynamism of modern African fine art, proving that moving into the global future begins with honoring the ancestral past.

Mediums & Materials

Raw Belgian Linen

Extra-heavy portrait-grade linen providing tactile tooth and archival resilience for dense sculptural paint layers.

Oil & Earth Pigments

Traditional cold-pressed oils blended with local volcanic charcoal and rich West African earth ochres.

24K Gold Leaf & Sizing

Genuine 24-karat gold leaf applied with historical gilding size to create enduring, shimmering depth that catches light.
